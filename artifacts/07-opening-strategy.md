# 07 Opening Strategy

## Opening Strategy
Open with Luna being left by her partner in a Lima terminal minutes before Gabriel sees her across the crowd, creating immediate dual collapse and unavoidable collision.

## First Line Options
1. "By the time the rain found her, Luna had already been left once that morning."
2. "Gabriel recognized his daughter by the way she refused to cry in public."
3. "The loudspeaker called final boarding as if endings were administrative."

## First Page Promise
A high-emotion, high-momentum reunion story where every chapter compounds intimacy risk.

## Chapter One Ending
Luna's ride-share is cancelled during transit strikes, Gabriel's hotel has one spare room, and she says yes only because she has nowhere else to go.
