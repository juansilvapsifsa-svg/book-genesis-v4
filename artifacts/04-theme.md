# 04 Theme

## Central Theme
Reconciliation requires accuracy before forgiveness.

## Theme as a Question
Can a parent and child love each other truthfully after years of mutually edited memory?

## Subthemes
- Abandonment echo across generations
- Pride as a slow form of self-harm
- Travel as dislocation that enables emotional honesty

## Symbols
- Altitude sickness as emotional truth surfacing
- Unsent letters as frozen love
- River crossings as thresholds of accountability
