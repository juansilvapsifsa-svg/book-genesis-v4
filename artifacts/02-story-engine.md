# 02 Story Engine

## Premise Expansion
After ending a long, corrosive relationship, Gabriel travels to Peru to scatter his mother's ashes along a route she once planned. In Lima, he unexpectedly encounters his estranged daughter, Luna, whose partner has just abandoned her mid-trip. Forced into proximity, they agree to travel together only until Cusco—then uncover letters revealing the lie that split them.

## Central Conflict
Can love survive after trust has been replaced by a family myth both people used to protect themselves?

## Escalation Logic
1. Coincidental reunion with mutual hostility.
2. Practical alliance due to safety/logistics.
3. Reopened wound: conflicting memory of the estrangement event.
4. Discovery of mother's unsent letters exposing third-party manipulation.
5. Public emotional rupture in Sacred Valley.
6. High-altitude crisis requiring literal dependence.
7. Final truth choice: confess fully and rebuild slowly, or separate forever with partial peace.

## Differentiation Strategy
- Every geographic move triggers a relational test.
- Dialogue built for quotability without flattening complexity.
- Romance breakups function as mirrors, not subplots.
