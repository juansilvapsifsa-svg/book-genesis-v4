# 03 Characters

## Gabriel Álvarez (Father, 52)
- Core Wound: Failed his daughter at the exact moment she asked to be believed.
- Conscious Desire: Earn one honest conversation with Luna before returning home.
- Unconscious Need: Stop performing dignity and accept visible remorse.
- Lie He Believes: "Protecting people from the whole truth is love."
- Contradictions: emotionally perceptive with strangers, emotionally evasive with family.
- Voice Notes: restrained, sensory, occasionally sardonic; avoids direct apology language.

## Luna Álvarez (Daughter, 28)
- Core Wound: Felt replaced by her father's new life after her mother died.
- Conscious Desire: Prove she never needed him.
- Unconscious Need: Mourn openly and ask for care without self-erasure.
- Lie She Believes: "If I need him, I lose myself."
- Contradictions: hyper-competent in crisis, self-destructive in intimacy.
- Voice Notes: fast, sharp, image-heavy; humor used as armor.

## Sofía Márquez (Absent Mother, present through letters)
- Core Wound: Managed conflict by curation instead of confrontation.
- Narrative Function: posthumous catalyst exposing memory distortion.
