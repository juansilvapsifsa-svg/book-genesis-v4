# 01 Market Map

## Market Signals
- Strong demand for intergenerational trauma fiction with redemptive arc.
- Social platforms reward quote-dense emotional lines and "healing journey" hooks.
- Travel-rich novels perform well when location shapes plot (not just scenery).

## Comp Titles
1. *Hello Beautiful* (Ann Napolitano)
2. *The Paper Palace* (Miranda Cowley Heller)
3. *One Italian Summer* (Rebecca Serle)
4. *All the Light We Cannot See* (for emotional memory architecture, not plot similarity)

## Recurring Patterns
- Fractured family secret revealed in staged disclosures
- Parallel collapse in adult relationships
- Climactic confession in a physically extreme setting

## Whitespace Opportunity
Position Peru not as backdrop but as moral engine: altitude, ritual, and pilgrimage physically pressure each character into truth before they lose each other again.
