# 00 Brief

## Original User Idea
Write a high-velocity, viral-potential novel about an estranged father and daughter who reconnect by chance in Peru after both romantic lives collapse.

## Inferred Direction
A dual-POV emotional road novel where each stop on a Peruvian journey forces a confrontation with shared family mythology and unreconciled harm.

- Language: English
- Genre: Upmarket commercial relationship fiction
- Audience: readers of *Hello Beautiful*, *The Paper Palace*, and destination-driven emotional fiction
- Target Length: 90,000-100,000 words
- Narrative Mode: dual close third with epistolary inserts
- Reader Promise: an addictive reconciliation story that blends lush travel immersion, painful family truth, and escalating emotional stakes.
