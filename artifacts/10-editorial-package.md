# 10 Editorial Package

## Logline
After being abandoned in Lima, a young woman is forced to travel across Peru with the estranged father she blames for her broken past—until hidden letters reveal they were both betrayed by the same family lie.

## Back-Cover Blurb
Luna Álvarez has just been left by the man she thought she would marry when she collides with the last person she wants to see: her father.

Gabriel Álvarez is in Peru to complete his late mother's final wish, carrying an urn and a silence that has lasted nearly a decade. Stranded by transit strikes and bad timing, father and daughter strike a temporary truce that turns into a journey through Lima, Cusco, and the high Andes—where every mile strips away one more version of the story they've told themselves.

When they uncover a cache of unsent letters that rewrites the night their family broke, Luna and Gabriel must decide whether truth can repair what love could not.

For readers who crave emotionally intense, impossible-to-put-down fiction, this is a story about the people we leave, the lies we inherit, and the terrifying intimacy of a second chance.

## Editorial Synopsis
Gabriel and Luna reunite by chance in Peru after simultaneous romantic collapse. Their reluctant joint travel exposes unresolved grief tied to Luna's mother and grandmother. Through discovered letters, they learn that the estrangement event was manipulated by selective omissions and redirected messages. The truth does not erase Gabriel's failures or Luna's resentment; instead, it reframes culpability and forces both into accountability. A high-altitude emergency compels practical interdependence, followed by a painful full-timeline confession. They end not with instant absolution but with a concrete trust practice and ongoing relational work.

## Cover Brief
- Positioning: upmarket emotional bestseller with cinematic travel scope.
- Imagery: father/daughter silhouettes on an Andean ridge; torn letter overlay; storm-to-gold palette shift.
- Tone keywords: aching, propulsive, intimate, redemptive.
- Reader signal: "if you loved character-driven stories that make you cry and text quotes to friends."

## Formatting Notes
- Ebook: linked TOC, short chapters optimized for mobile reading cadence.
- Print: 5.5x8.5 trim, generous leading for readability, section-break ornaments tied to map motifs.
- Back matter: reading-group questions + author note on Peru research ethics.

## Query Strategy
- Hook order: breakup collision -> estranged reunion -> Peru journey -> hidden letters.
- Position as upmarket with book-club crossover and adaptation-friendly structure.
- Avoid certainty claims (e.g., guaranteed bestseller); emphasize reader compulsion and emotional aftertaste.
