# 05 Outline

## Macro Structure
- Act I (Collision): accidental reunion and reluctant alliance.
- Act II (Excavation): letters, contradictory memories, and emotional sabotage.
- Act III (Exposure): physical and emotional extremis leading to truth contract.

## Narrative Function per Block
- 1-3 hook and chemistry under hostility.
- 4-7 deepen interiority and plausibility of repair.
- 8-11 fracture midpoint and escalate stakes.
- 12-15 force vulnerability through survival conditions.
- 16-18 deliver emotionally specific, non-sentimental resolution.

## Central Conflict
Truth versus self-protection inside a father-daughter bond.

## Ending Turn
They choose a practice of honesty (monthly recorded conversations) over symbolic instant forgiveness.

## Tension Map
Spark -> Friction -> Warmth -> Detonation -> Survival -> Confession -> Earned soft landing.
